# Getting support

- **Questions and ideas**: use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hermeto/aibox/discussions).
- **Bugs**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/hermeto/aibox/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature requests**: open a [feature request](https://github.com/hermeto/aibox/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Documentation problems**: open a [docs issue](https://github.com/hermeto/aibox/issues/new?template=docs.yml).
- **Security issues**: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — never in public.

AIBox is a volunteer, single-maintainer project; responses are best effort. There is no
commercial support.
